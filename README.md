# 🌿 Clovera — Grow Your World

**Clovera** is a plant-focused platform designed to help people discover the joy of plants, find the perfect plants for their lifestyle, and learn how to care for them.

Our goal is to make plant discovery and plant care simple, enjoyable, and personalized.

## 🌱 Our Features

### 🪴 1. Plant Shop
Explore a variety of plants and discover options that suit your preferences, lifestyle, and space.

### 💬 2. Plant Care Chatbot
Get guidance and answers to your plant care questions, including watering, sunlight, soil, and common plant problems.

### 🌿 3. Find Your Perfect Plant
Discover plants that match your environment, available space, lighting conditions, experience level, and personal preferences.

### ✨ 4. Plant Personality Test
Take a fun, interactive quiz to discover which plant best matches your personality and lifestyle.

### 👤 5. Personalized User Experience
Enjoy a personalized experience that helps you explore relevant plants and find recommendations suited to your needs.

### 🔐 6. User Accounts
Create an account and log in to enjoy a more personalized Clovera experience.

## 🌍 Our Vision

We believe everyone can create a greener, more beautiful space. Clovera aims to make choosing and caring for plants easier for beginners and plant lovers alike.

**Clovera — Grow Your World, One Plant at a Time.** 🌱
